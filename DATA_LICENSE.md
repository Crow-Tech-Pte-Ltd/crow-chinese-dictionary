# Dictionary data licences

Crow Chinese Dictionary is an assembled and edited dataset. Compatible derived
records use CC BY-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ .
This does not change the original licences of upstream raw sources. Every exported
record must retain its provenance, author attribution and source licence links.
MIT source material also retains its full copyright and permission notice.

This repository is not a copy of ABC, Oxford, New Century or other proprietary
dictionaries. Source headword and sense coverage is measured against separately
identified, openly licensed materials supplied by Hanzi Desk.

The Ministry of Education CC BY-ND sources are discovery-only private leads.
Their definitions are excluded from prompts, derived glosses and public exports.
Their inclusion in a reference-universe count does not mean they are redistributable
Crow entries. A lead needs independent, permissible attestation before export.

Mixed-licence WordNet packs require component-by-component review before enabling
that evidence for a public derivative. Merely finding a phrase in several translated
WordNet packs is not independent corroboration.

Model-written entries are labelled as drafts or machine-checked. They are never
labelled human-reviewed without a genuine human decision tied to the exact draft
and evidence hashes. A generated paraphrase is not automatically fully original
or free of upstream share-alike obligations.

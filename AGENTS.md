# Crow Chinese Dictionary

- Keep the Hanzi Desk website and native-app checkouts read-only while building this dataset.
- No production integration, publish, push or corpus audio regeneration without user authorization.
- Never read unrelated secrets. Consume only explicitly supplied API key paths through provider clients; never print/copy them into this repository.
- Keys, account/quota records, raw API responses, work databases and unreviewed drafts remain ignored under private/ or work/.
- Word identity includes spoken variety, canonical spelling with evidence-backed aliases, and lexical reading. Do not collapse polyphonic readings or Mandarin/Cantonese.
- Only unique source-attested readings may resolve blanks. Do not have a model invent pinyin or Jyutping.
- Candidate discovery does not prove lexical correctness, a new sense, or absence from every dictionary worldwide.
- ND definitions must never enter model prompts or public derived records. Preserve per-record licence attribution.
- Generator and checker are separate roles. Same-model review cannot establish an independent-model approval; automatic review never becomes human review.
- Preserve the persistent budget ledger across retries, restarts and workers. Do not reset usage to evade caps or silently switch provider/account/model on failure.
- Do not claim complete dictionary or audio coverage while held/unresolved/failed/unverified entries remain.
